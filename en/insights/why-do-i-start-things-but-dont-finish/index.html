Why do I start things but don't finish?

Strong launch energy and weak sustain are two different systems.

You may get activated by novelty, then lose traction once tasks become repetitive or delayed in feedback.

This pattern often appears in Explorer (XOS) and Multi-Version (MXT).

Related types: Explorer (XOS) · Multi-Version (MXT)

Try these 3 steps first

1. Set a clear stop condition.

2. Ship a small version before refining.

3. Use real feedback instead of mental simulation.
