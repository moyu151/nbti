Why do I feel like different versions of myself?

Changing by context can be adaptive, not inauthentic.

You may shift expression, pace, and social intensity across scenes while still maintaining a core inner logic.

This is common in Multi-Version (MXT) and Adaptive Generalist (SACE).

Related types: Multi-Version (MXT) · Adaptive Generalist (SACE)

Try these 3 steps first

1. Set a clear stop condition.

2. Ship a small version before refining.

3. Use real feedback instead of mental simulation.
