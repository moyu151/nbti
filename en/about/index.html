NBTI

NBTI does not define who you are.

It describes how you currently operate.

What NBTI is doing

We are not trying to lock you into a label.

We focus on your operating pattern:

How you decide.

How you start, and where you stall.

How you relate to people.

Where you lose energy repeatedly.

Why it often feels accurate

Many tests ask identity-first questions like "Are you extroverted?"

NBTI asks behavior-first questions like:

"Do you know what to do, but still fail to start?"

"Do you replay small events repeatedly in your mind?"

"Can you chat well, but dislike initiating social contact?"

When these patterns are described in sequence, people often feel seen.

What NBTI is not

NBTI is not a clinical diagnosis.

It is not a permanent identity verdict.

It is a snapshot of the behavior strategy you are using more often right now.

How the system works

Layer 1: Questions. You answer "Does this feel like me?" in real scenarios.

Layer 2: Dimensions. Answers are aggregated into behavior tendencies.

Layer 3: Type output. Tendencies are composed into a readable personality mode.

Final line

You are not a single-version person.

NBTI helps you see which version is running most often now.
