About NBTI

NBTI does not define who you are. It describes how you currently operate.

Most personality tests ask identity labels first. NBTI starts from real behavior patterns: how you start, where you stall, and how you adapt in relationships and work.

That is why NBTI often feels “accurate”: it maps lived micro-patterns instead of abstract traits only.

NBTI is not a diagnosis. It is a practical, shareable self-reflection system.
