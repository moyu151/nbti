Interpretation Boundaries

Is NBTI a clinical diagnosis?

No. NBTI is a reflection and communication tool. It is not medical, psychiatric, or legal advice.

Can my type change?

Yes. Your dominant operating pattern can shift with context, stress, and life stage.

Why does NBTI feel accurate?

Because it describes repeated real behaviors, not only abstract labels.
