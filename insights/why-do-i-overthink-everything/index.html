Why Do I Overthink Everything?

Overthinking is often a strategy, not a flaw.

If you replay conversations, simulate outcomes, and delay action until things feel certain, your system may prioritize safety over speed.

This often maps to Overthinker (EXS) and sometimes overlaps with Emotional Radar (SEA).

What helps

1. Set a thinking deadline.

2. Take one small action before full certainty.

3. Use feedback to replace endless simulation.
