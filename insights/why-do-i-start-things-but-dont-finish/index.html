Why Do I Start Things but Never Finish?

Starting fast and sustaining long are different systems.

Many people are strong at ignition and weak at continuation. That is not laziness; it is a novelty-biased strategy.

Common related types: Explorer (XOS), Multi-Version (MXT), and EXS.

What helps

1. Keep one active main project.

2. Park new ideas in an idea queue.

3. Break completion into short visible stages.
