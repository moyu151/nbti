Why Do I Feel Like Different Versions of Myself?

Context switching can look like inconsistency, but it is often adaptive intelligence.

If your behavior shifts by context, group, and role, you may be running a multi-version strategy.

Often related to Multi-Version (MXT) and sometimes Adaptive Integrator (SACE).

What helps

1. Define core rules that do not change.

2. Let style change, keep values stable.

3. Review where switching is useful vs draining.
